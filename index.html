Getting started

  1. Set a focus-session length and start the timer. The timer counts elapsed time; it cannot verify your attention.
  2. Completed focus time earns points. Open Blocked apps from the menu to choose apps, categories, or websites and set your daily blocking window.
  3. Allow Screen Time access if you want iOS to apply blocking. Your choice is voluntary. The daily window must be at least 15 minutes, and a real points-funded unlock is at least 15 minutes (75 points).

Stop or change blocking

Open Blocked apps and choose Stop blocking now to stop the schedule and clear Rise Again's shields and monitors. You can also change your selection or schedule there. To revoke permission in iOS, open Settings and find Rise Again's Screen Time access; the exact Settings wording may vary by iOS version. Rise Again stops its blocking when it detects that access was revoked.

Permission troubleshooting

  • If access was declined or revoked, return to Blocked apps and request Screen Time access again, or review the app's permission in iOS Settings.
  • Make sure at least one app, category, or website is selected and that the daily start and end times are different and at least 15 minutes apart.
  • Confirm the schedule is active and try refreshing or reopening the app. iOS controls monitoring callbacks, so a scheduled change or unlock may be applied later than its exact target time.
  • If permission is unavailable or an error persists, include your iOS version and a description of what happened when you contact support. Do not email passwords or sensitive information.
