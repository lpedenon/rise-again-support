About this policy

This policy describes the Rise Again app and these public support and privacy pages. The app and the website have different data practices, described separately below.

Publisher: Leon Pedenon
Privacy contact: pedenonleon@gmail.com

Information handled by the app

  • Focus-session and collection progress, points, and schedule choices are stored locally on your device.
  • If you choose Screen Time features, the app saves your Family Controls selection as Apple's opaque tokens and shares the necessary selection and schedule state with its app extension using Apple's app-group storage. The app does not extract readable names of selected apps, categories, or websites from these tokens.
  • The app reads Apple's Screen Time authorization status to determine whether it can apply shields. Permission is voluntary and can be denied or revoked.

In the app source reviewed for this release, there is no account system, developer analytics, advertising, crash-reporting integration, or developer-server collection of this app data. The app does not provide cloud storage or account-based recovery. Apple's operating-system services implement Screen Time and are subject to Apple's own terms and privacy information.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline Screen Time permission, change your selection, or use the app's Stop blocking now control. When the app detects revoked authorization, it stops its monitoring and clears its app-owned shields. You can reset the app's demo progress in its settings. Uninstalling or resetting the app may affect locally stored app data. These controls do not delete email you have already sent or data handled independently by Apple.

Support website and email

If you choose to email the publisher, your email address and message are sent through your email provider and the recipient's email service. Please include only information needed to answer your request.

These pages are hosted with GitHub Pages. As with ordinary website hosting, GitHub may receive technical request information when a browser requests a page. GitHub's handling of that information is governed by GitHub's terms and privacy statement. This site does not add cookies, analytics, tracking scripts, or a contact form.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated if the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. Questions can be sent to pedenonleon@gmail.com.
